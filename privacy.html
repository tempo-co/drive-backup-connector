Privacy policy

Home · Privacy policy

This policy covers the private-use Drive Backup Connector and these information pages.

Information processed

The connector processes encrypted backup files and Google Drive metadata, such as file identifiers, names, sizes, checksums and account storage usage. OAuth credentials authorize access to the Google Drive account selected by its owner.

Purpose and storage

This information is used only to create, verify, restore and rotate backups. Backup contents are encrypted before upload and stored in the authorized Google Drive account. Credentials and recovery secrets are managed privately by the backup operator, not by this website.

Sharing and use

The connector does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising or use it to train AI models. Google provides the destination storage and processes data under its own terms and privacy policy. This site is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose hosting infrastructure may process standard website request information under the GitHub privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

Backup retention is configured by the backup operator. The account owner can remove stored backups through Google Drive or ask the operator to remove them. Google Drive trash and deletion behavior may affect when storage is reclaimed.

Revoking authorization

Access can be revoked in Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future access by the connector but does not delete existing Drive files or local copies. The operator can remove the connector's local credentials when access is no longer needed.

This website and contact

These pages contain no forms, analytics scripts or advertising trackers, and do not collect Google credentials or backup uploads. For questions about an individual backup or a data request, contact the person who configured it. For questions about these published pages, use the project issue tracker without including private information.
