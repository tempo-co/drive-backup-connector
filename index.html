Drive Backup Connector

Home · Privacy policy

A private-use utility for storing encrypted backups in a Google Drive account authorized by its owner. This is not a public backup service.

What it does

The connector uploads, lists, downloads and verifies encrypted backup files. It also deletes obsolete backup files according to the retention policy configured by the backup operator.

Google Drive access

Google authorization is used to manage backup files and read storage usage in the authorized Drive account. The connector does not request Gmail, Calendar or Contacts access.

Backup contents are encrypted before upload. This website does not provide account sign-in, accept backup uploads or store Google credentials.

Control and support

The account owner controls access and retention. Access can be revoked in Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future access by the connector; it does not delete backups already stored in Drive.

For help with an individual backup, contact the person who configured it. Documentation questions can be raised in the project issue tracker. Do not include private data or credentials.
